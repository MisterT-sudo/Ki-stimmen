Frequently asked questions

Is this a real voice actor?

No, we use a high-quality AI voice. That makes us fast and affordable – and changes are possible within 24 hours at any time, without a new studio session.

Will it work with my phone system?

Almost always. We deliver the file in the format your system needs (e.g. MP3 or WAV). Just tell us which system you have – or we will help you upload it.

Who writes the script?

We do. You just give us the key facts (opening hours, cover, special notes) and you get the script for approval in advance.

What if I don't like the announcement?

One round of revisions is always included. We adjust the wording or emphasis until it fits.
